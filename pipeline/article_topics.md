## Before you publish: claims to verify

- **Search volume is unmeasured.** Every keyword is a phrase people are demonstrably targeting in search results, not a verified volume. Check the head terms in Google Trends and Keyword Planner first.
- **Topics marked "⚠ Confirm first" depend on facts about TAB Explorer I don't have.** Do not publish those claims until you've checked them in the actual extension: local-only storage, no account, price, permissions, Edge/Brave behavior, keyboard shortcuts, import from other tools.
- **Re-check every competitor fact on the day you publish.** Features, pricing and free-plan limits change often, and wrong claims about a named competitor damage trust.
- **Don't promise measured results** such as RAM savings or minutes saved unless you've tested them yourself.
- **Niche articles must not give legal, medical or financial advice.** They are about organizing information.
